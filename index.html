CITY EXPLORER

From morning chai to midnight madness — the best spots in Bangkok, Pattaya & Phuket.

🏯

Bangkok

City of Angels · Population 10M · GMT+7 (ICT)

The city that never sleeps, never judges, and never disappoints. Streets full of stories.

🗓️ 1 Night Stay · Jan 13–14

🗺️ Must-Visit Tourist Spots

🏯
Grand Palace

Thailand's most iconic landmark. The royal palace complex with Wat Phra Kaew inside. Breathtaking.

Must-See
⛩️
Wat Arun

Temple of Dawn on the Chao Phraya river. Even better at sunset — golden hour magic.

Sunset Spot
🛺
Khao San Road

The legendary backpacker street. Drinks, street food, live music — infinite chaos all night.

Party Zone
🛍️
Chatuchak Weekend Market

8,000 stalls! Asia's largest weekend market. Find anything from antiques to streetwear.

Shopping

☕ Coffee Shops & Vibes

☕
Roots Coffee Roaster

Bangkok's specialty coffee pioneer. Single-origin beans, pour-overs, beautiful space.

Specialty
🏭
Factory Coffee

Industrial-chic roastery in Silom area. Excellent espresso and killer cold brew.

Trendy
🌿
Ceresia Coffee Roasters

Hidden gem cafe with great pour-overs and a cozy, art-filled atmosphere.

Hidden Gem

🎊 Nightlife & Party Spots

🎉
Levels Club & Lounge

Sukhumvit's most popular multi-floor club. 3 floors, different vibes. Book ahead!

Top Club
🎵
Onyx Nightclub

Bangkok's superclub. World-class DJs, massive LED dance floor, insane production.

Superclub
🌆
Sky Bar (Lebua Hotel)

The Hangover movie's rooftop bar! Incredible 360° city views. Dress code enforced.

Iconic
🏖️

Pattaya

Party City · 150km from Bangkok · GMT+7 (ICT)

The city that literally wrote the rulebook on bachelor trips. No filter needed here.

🗓️ 3 Nights Stay · Jan 14–16

🗺️ Must-Visit Tourist Spots

🏝️
Coral Island (Koh Larn)

Crystal clear waters, 6 beautiful beaches, amazing snorkeling. 20-min speedboat ride.

Top Pick
🏛️
Sanctuary of Truth

All-wood temple masterpiece, 105m tall. Carved with mythological figures. Breathtaking.

Landmark
🌳
Nong Nooch Garden

Stunning botanical garden. Elephant shows, cactus garden, amazing Thai cultural shows.

Nature
🏄
Pattaya Beach

Jet skiing, parasailing, banana boats. Full beach scene all day long.

Water Sports

☕ Coffee Shops & Vibes

🎨
Art in Paradise Cafe

3D art museum with amazing coffee. Perfect for fun group photos with wild optical illusions!

📸 Insta-worthy
☁️
Coffee Clouds

Elevated coffee experience. Great views, perfect for recovering with a cold brew morning.

Views
🌊
Surf House Cafe

Coffee + indoor surfing simulator! Grab a smoothie and ride the wave.

Unique

🎊 Nightlife & Party Spots

🌃
Walking Street

Southeast Asia's most famous party strip. 1km of pure madness after 9PM. Unmissable.

Legendary
😈
Lucifer Disco

Pattaya's oldest and most iconic club on Walking Street. Dance till the sun comes up!

Icon
🎸
Mojo Bar Pattaya

Brilliant live music bar. Rock bands, cold beers, loud fun — the vibe is immaculate.

Live Music
🌴

Phuket

Pearl of the Andaman Sea · Island Paradise · GMT+7 (ICT)

The grand finale. Thailand's crown jewel. The island that earns its legendary reputation every night.

🗓️ 2 Nights Stay · Jan 16–19

🗺️ Must-Visit Tourist Spots

🏝️
Phi Phi Islands

THE most beautiful islands in Thailand. Maya Bay from "The Beach" movie. Full-day boat tour.

Bucket List
🗿
Big Buddha

45m white marble Buddha statue on Nakkerd Hill. Panoramic views of the whole island.

Landmark
⛵
Phang Nga Bay

James Bond Island! Dramatic limestone karsts rising from emerald water. Sea caves, kayaking.

Iconic
🏖️
Patong Beach

Most lively beach in Phuket. Crystal waters, watersports galore, beachside cocktail bars.

Beach Party

☕ Coffee Shops & Vibes

🚪
Yellow Door Cafe

Charming cafe in Phuket Old Town's colorful streets. Beautiful decor, fantastic Thai iced coffee.

📸 Iconic
🏠
Shelter Coffee

Best specialty cold brew in Phuket. Cozy, Patong area, the vibes are immaculate.

Specialty
🌿
Cherngtalay Coffee

Hipster-chic cafe near Laguna area. Great for slow morning sessions. Excellent pour-overs.

Chill Zone

🎊 Nightlife & Party Spots

🌃
Bangla Road

Phuket's answer to Walking Street. The strip you absolutely CANNOT miss. It's something else.

The Strip
💡
Illuzion Nightclub

Phuket's largest club — 5000 capacity. World-class sound and light show. Pure spectacle.

Mega Club
🐯
Tiger Nightclub

Multi-level entertainment complex on Bangla. Live shows, rooftop bar, main floor mayhem.

Must Visit
