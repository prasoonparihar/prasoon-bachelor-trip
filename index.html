01
WED, 13 JAN 2027 · BANGKOK (1 NIGHT STAY)
🛫 Red-Eye Flight from BLR to Bangkok & Old Town Exploration
🏯

Depart Kempegowda Airport at 02:00 IST on IndiGo 6E-1213. Touch down in Bangkok Suvarnabhumi at 07:20 ICT. Check in, grab strong Thai roast coffee, and hit the historic Grand Palace & sunset at Wat Arun before the first legendary night on Khao San Road!

02
THU, 14 JAN 2027 · PATTAYA (3 NIGHTS STAY)
🚗 Bangkok to Pattaya — Highway 7 Road Trip Express!
🛣️

Morning breakfast and cold brews in Bangkok. Pack bags and embark on the ~150 km road trip down the Eastern Seaboard via Highway 7. Pitbull blasted on full volume. Check in to Pattaya beach resort, watch the sea sunset, and plunge headfirst into Walking Street!

03
FRI, 15 JAN 2027 · PATTAYA DAY 2
🏖️ Coral Island Speedboat Safari & The Sanctuary of Truth
🌊

Speedboat ride to Koh Larn (Coral Island) for parasailing, jet skiing, and swimming in crystal turquoise waters. Afternoon visit to the jaw-dropping hand-carved all-wood Sanctuary of Truth. Recover with a 90-minute traditional Thai massage before live music at Mojo Bar!

🚤 09:30 Speedboat to Koh Larn 🤿 Snorkeling Coral Reefs 🏄 Parasailing over the Bay 🥥 Fresh Coconut on Tien Beach 🏛️ Sanctuary of Truth Architectural Tour 💆 90-min Deep Tissue Thai Massage ☁️ Coffee Clouds Espresso Bar 🎸 Mojo Bar Live Rock Session 🌃 Walking Street Round 2

SAT, 16 JAN 2027 · FLIGHT TO PHUKET (2 NIGHTS STAY)
✈️ Pattaya to Don Mueang ➔ Fly to Phuket Paradise!
🌴

Morning drive from Pattaya to Bangkok Don Mueang Airport (DMK). Board Thai AirAsia FD3017 at 15:15 ICT. Land in Phuket at 16:50 ICT. Check in to Patong resort, watch the Andaman sunset, and experience the legendary sensory overload of Bangla Road!

05
SUN, 17 JAN 2027 · PHUKET DAY 2
🛥️ Phi Phi Islands Catamaran Cruise & Big Buddha
🏝️

Full-day luxury catamaran cruise to the Phi Phi Islands! Swim in Maya Bay (where Leonardo DiCaprio filmed The Beach), snorkel alongside tropical fish, explore Viking Cave, and head to the top of Nakkerd Hill for 360-degree views from the Big Buddha statue.

🛥️ Phi Phi Islands Catamaran Cruise 🤿 Maya Bay Snorkeling & Swimming ⛵ Viking Cave & Pileh Lagoon 🗿 Big Buddha Nakkerd Hill Panoramic View 🚪 Yellow Door Cafe Phuket Old Town 🛍️ Jungceylon Night Bazaar 🦞 Beachfront Grilled Lobster Feast 🐯 Tiger Nightclub Bangla Road
06
MON, 18 JAN 2027 · PHUKET DAY 3
🍾 The Grand Bachelor Finale — The Ultimate Hurrah!
👑

The final full day in Thailand! A day of chill recovery, specialty cold brew coffee at Shelter, an ultra-luxurious spa session, and the official Bachelor Farewell Royal Dinner. Tonight we party till sunrise — the last wild night before Prasoon steps onto the wedding stage!

🏖️ Patong Beach Cabana Session 🏠 Shelter Coffee Nitro Cold Brew 💆 Royal Aromatherapy Spa Package 🌿 Cherngtalay Chill Zone 🍾 Official Groom's Farewell Toast 🎤 Shameless Karaoke Battles 🌃 Bangla Road Grand Finale Invasion 🌅 Watch the Final Andaman Sunrise Together

🛬 Akasa Air QP-624 Flight Home — Mission Accomplished! 💍
👑

Final morning mango sticky rice, last souvenir hunting in Phuket Old Town, and transfer to Phuket International Airport. Board Akasa Air QP-624 at 17:00 ICT. Land back at Bengaluru T2 at 19:10 IST. Groom delivered safe, sound, and ready to get married!
